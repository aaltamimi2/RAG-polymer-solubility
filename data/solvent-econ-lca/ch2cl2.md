# Dichloromethane (Methylene Chloride)

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 75-09-2
- **Common names**: Methylene chloride, CH₂Cl₂, DCM
- **Chemical class**: Chlorinated aliphatic hydrocarbon, halogenated solvent

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $0.27-0.95 (regional variation) | Source: ChemAnalyst, IMARC Group (Oct 2025)
  - Northeast Asia: $0.27/kg
  - North America: $0.62/kg
  - Europe: $0.80-0.95/kg
  - Germany: $0.92/kg (Sep 2025)
- **Price confidence**: High
- **Notes**: Significant regional price variation driven by demand from pharmaceuticals, paint stripping, and foam-blowing applications. Northeast Asia shows lowest pricing due to subdued demand and limited export opportunities. European prices reflect higher production costs and stricter regulations.

- **GWP (kg CO₂-eq/kg)**: Not found in search results | Source: Data not available from public sources
- **HTC (CTUh/kg)**: Not quantified | Source: USEtox methodology available but specific values not published
- **HTNC (CTUh/kg)**: Not quantified | Source: USEtox methodology available but specific values not published
- **ETOX (CTUe/kg)**: Not quantified | Source: CTUe calculation framework exists but specific values not found
- **LCA confidence**: Low
- **LCA methodology**: EPA considers dichloromethane a probable human carcinogen (Group B2). IARC classifies it as possibly carcinogenic (Group 2B). USEtox model provides framework for characterization factors but specific production LCA data from ecoinvent not found in public literature. Environmental fate and ecotoxicity studies indicate low acute toxicity to aquatic organisms.

## Summary
Dichloromethane is a widely-used chlorinated solvent with bulk industrial prices ranging from $0.27-0.95/kg depending on region. It is classified as a probable human carcinogen with neurotoxicity and liver effects, though comprehensive cradle-to-gate LCA impact factors (GWP, CTUh, CTUe) were not available from public databases or literature searches.
