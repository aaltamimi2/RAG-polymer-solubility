# Methanol

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 67-56-1
- **Common names**: Methanol, methyl alcohol, wood alcohol, MeOH
- **Chemical class**: Primary alcohol (simplest aliphatic alcohol)

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $0.34-0.74 | Source: January 2026 regional spot prices
- **Price confidence**: High
- **Notes**: Strong regional price variation. South America: $0.34/kg, Northeast Asia: $0.35/kg, Africa: $0.36/kg, Middle East: $0.36/kg, Southeast Asia: $0.39/kg, Europe: $0.65/kg, North America: $0.74/kg. Market showed downward pressure in Q4 2025-Q1 2026 due to economic uncertainties and limited industrial consumption. High-volume commodity with transparent global pricing.

## Environmental Impact Factors (LCA)
- **GWP (kg CO₂-eq/kg)**: 0.94-2.20 | Source: Natural gas-based production LCA studies
- **HTC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found (specific values) | Source: USEtox methodology available but compound-specific data not identified
- **HTNC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found (specific values) | Source: USEtox methodology available but compound-specific data not identified
- **ETOX (CTUe/kg)**: Not found | Source: No specific ecotoxicity characterization factor identified
- **LCA confidence**: High (for GWP), Low (for toxicity metrics)
- **LCA methodology**: Multiple cradle-to-gate LCA studies using ecoinvent 3.8-3.9. GWP varies by production route: traditional steam methane reforming (SMR) = 0.944 kg CO₂-eq/kg; state-of-art natural gas = 2.05-2.20 kg CO₂-eq/kg; with CO₂ recycling = 1.86-2.02 kg CO₂-eq/kg (93-101 g CO₂-eq/MJ); coal-based = 2.97 kg CO₂-eq/kg; renewable routes can achieve negative emissions

## Summary
Methanol is the simplest alcohol and a high-volume commodity chemical with well-established global markets and pricing. Production GWP well-documented (0.94-2.97 kg CO₂-eq/kg depending on feedstock), but human toxicity and ecotoxicity LCA metrics are not widely reported despite known acute toxicity to humans (optic nerve damage, blindness).
