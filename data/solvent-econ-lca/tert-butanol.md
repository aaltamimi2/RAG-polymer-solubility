# tert-Butanol

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 75-65-0
- **Common names**: tert-Butyl alcohol, 2-methyl-2-propanol, TBA, tertiary butyl alcohol
- **Chemical class**: Tertiary alcohol

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: 0.74 - 1.31 USD/kg (regional variation, October 2025) | Source: [IMARC Group](https://www.imarcgroup.com/tertiary-butyl-alcohol-pricing-report), [ChemAnalyst](https://www.chemanalyst.com/Pricing-data/tertiary-butyl-alcohol-1141)
  - Northeast Asia: 0.74 USD/kg
  - Africa: 0.99 USD/kg
  - Europe: 0.97 - 1.71 USD/kg (Q3 2025 average: 1.71 USD/kg)
  - North America: 1.07 USD/kg
  - South America: 1.31 USD/kg
- **Price confidence**: Medium
- **Notes**: Prices updated monthly, show regional variation driven by feedstock costs, logistics, and downstream MTBE blending/solvent demand. Europe typically commands premium pricing. China market price approximately 0.92 USD/kg in September 2025.

## Toxicity and Environmental Behavior
- **Aquatic toxicity**: Goldfish (fresh water) death at 1000 ppm/24H; fathead minnow LC50=1940 mg/L/1H; aquatic plant toxicity 8500 ppm; bacteria inhibition 650 mg/L | Source: [NCBI](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9889776/)
- **Human exposure**: Poorly absorbed through skin, rapidly absorbed via inhalation or ingestion | Source: [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20722584/), [EPA Toxicological Review](https://iris.epa.gov/static/pdfs/1036tr.pdf)
- **Environmental fate**: Expected to biodegrade when released to soil, leach to groundwater or volatilize; low bioconcentration potential | Source: [Carl Roth SDS](https://www.carlroth.com/downloads/sdb/en/A/SDB_AE16_MT_EN.pdf)

## Summary
tert-Butanol is a tertiary alcohol used as a solvent and gasoline additive, with bulk industrial prices ranging from 0.74 to 1.31 USD/kg depending on region (October 2025). Limited public LCA data available, though biodegradable with low bioconcentration potential; specific GWP and toxicity characterization factors require access to commercial databases like ecoinvent.
