# p-Xylene (1,4-Dimethylbenzene)

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 106-42-3
- **Common names**: p-Xylene, para-xylene, 1,4-dimethylbenzene, 1,4-xylene
- **Chemical class**: Aromatic hydrocarbon, monocyclic aromatic

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $0.85-$1.27 | Source: [IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/1-4-dimethylbenzene-pricing-report) / [Intratec](https://www.intratec.us/chemical-markets/pxylene-price)
- **Price confidence**: Medium
- **Notes**: Middle East pricing at $0.85/kg (2023); Q4 2023 pricing reached $0.977/kg (977 USD/MT) in United States, $0.958/kg in South Korea, and $1.266/kg (1266 USD/MT) in Germany. Pricing varies regionally and correlates with crude oil prices and supply-demand dynamics in aromatics markets. p-Xylene is primarily used as feedstock for terephthalic acid (PTA) production.

## Environmental Impact Factors (LCA)
- **GWP (kg CO₂-eq/kg)**: ~2.0-2.5 (estimated from BTX production) | Source: [ACS Publication](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acssuschemeng.2c05417)
- **HTC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found | Source: USEtox database not accessible
- **HTNC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found | Source: USEtox database not accessible
- **ETOX (CTUe/kg)**: Not found | Source: USEtox database not accessible
- **LCA confidence**: Low-Medium
- **LCA methodology**: Literature indicates energy and GHG emissions for platform hydrocarbons including xylenes range from 37 MJ/kg and 0.4 kg CO₂-e/kg (small molecules like methanol) to 128 MJ/kg and 2.3 kg CO₂-e/kg for larger aromatics. Terephthalic acid (downstream product from p-xylene) has cradle-to-gate emissions of 2.4-3.1 kg CO₂-equiv/kg. Bio-based p-xylene from sugar cane can achieve 87% GWP reduction versus petroleum-based routes.

## Summary
p-Xylene (CAS 106-42-3) is a colorless aromatic hydrocarbon primarily used as feedstock for terephthalic acid and PET production. Bulk industrial prices range $0.85-$1.27/kg based on Q4 2023 data with significant regional variations. Estimated GWP is ~2.0-2.5 kg CO₂-eq/kg based on literature for aromatic hydrocarbon production, though specific cradle-to-gate LCA data for p-xylene was not found. Human toxicity and ecotoxicity characterization factors require specialized database access (USEtox, ecoinvent).
