# N-Methyl-2-pyrrolidone (NMP)

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 872-50-4
- **Common names**: NMP, N-Methylpyrrolidone, 1-Methyl-2-pyrrolidinone
- **Chemical class**: Polar aprotic solvent, cyclic amide

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: Not found in search results | Source: Data not publicly available
- **Price confidence**: Low
- **Notes**: Market valued at $1.19B (2025) growing to $1.59B (2029). US NMP Price Index rose in Q3 2025 driven by 3.0% CPI increase and rising benzene feedstock prices. Regional pricing varies significantly. ChemAnalyst and other databases track pricing but specific $/kg values not disclosed in search results. Primary applications include battery manufacturing (cathode electrode solvent), polymer dissolution, and petrochemical processing.

## Environmental Impact Factors (LCA)
- **GWP (kg CO₂-eq/kg)**: 3.11 (in polyurethane coating process) | Source: ACS Sustainable Chemistry & Engineering (ecoinvent v3.8)
- **HTC (CTUh/kg)**: Not quantified (reproductive toxicant H360D) | Source: EPA risk evaluation; USEtox framework applicable
- **HTNC (CTUh/kg)**: Not quantified (developmental toxicity) | Source: Benchmark dose modeling; human equivalent air concentration 350-490 ppm
- **ETOX (CTUe/kg)**: Low | Source: CICAD 35 - low acute toxicity to aquatic organisms and birds, biodegradable, low bioconcentration
- **LCA confidence**: Medium
- **LCA methodology**: Ecoinvent v3.8 and v3.10 contain NMP production data. In comparative cradle-to-gate LCA of polyurethane processes (CML 2001, ReCiPe Midpoint H), NMP process showed GWP of 3.11 kg CO₂-eq with highest impact across all categories compared to acetone, melt, and flow processes. Similar bio-based dispersants show 6-8 kg CO₂-eq/kg. NMP is restricted in several countries due to environmental and reproductive health impacts. EPA classified it as presenting unreasonable risk to human health primarily for developmental effects (fetal loss, reduced fertility).

## Summary
N-Methyl-2-pyrrolidone is a high-performance polar aprotic solvent critical to battery and polymer industries with an estimated production GWP of ~3.1 kg CO₂-eq/kg. It is classified as a reproductive toxicant (may damage unborn child) with low ecotoxicity but faces increasing regulatory restrictions. Bulk pricing data was not publicly available but market size and growth indicate strong industrial demand.
