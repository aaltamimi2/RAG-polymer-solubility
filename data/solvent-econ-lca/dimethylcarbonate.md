# Dimethyl Carbonate

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 616-38-6
- **Common names**: DMC, carbonic acid dimethyl ester
- **Chemical class**: Organic carbonate ester, formula (CH₃O)₂CO

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $0.60-$0.76 | Source: [ChemAnalyst Q3 2025 pricing data](https://www.chemanalyst.com/Pricing-data/di-methyl-carbonate-1276)
- **Price confidence**: High
- **Notes**: September 2025: China $601/MT ($0.60/kg), Netherlands CFR Rotterdam $760.67/MT ($0.76/kg). Q3 2025 APAC prices rose 0.52% QoQ driven by battery/EV demand. North America experienced softer prices due to oversupply. Europe saw stronger upward trend from logistics bottlenecks. Market valued at $937.7M (2025) projected to $1.80B by 2030.

## Summary
Dimethyl carbonate is a green solvent and methylating agent widely used in lithium-ion batteries, paints, coatings, and adhesives. It has well-established bulk industrial pricing ($0.60-0.76/kg) and partial LCA data showing significant GWP variation (1-3 kg CO₂-eq/kg) depending on production technology, with CO₂ utilization routes offering environmental advantages over conventional processes.
