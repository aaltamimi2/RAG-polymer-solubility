# Butanone (Methyl Ethyl Ketone)

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 78-93-3
- **Common names**: Methyl ethyl ketone (MEK), 2-butanone, Ethyl methyl ketone, Butan-2-one
- **Chemical class**: Aliphatic ketone
- **Molecular formula**: C4H8O
- **Molecular weight**: 72.11 g/mol
- **EC Number**: 201-159-0

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $1.20 - $1.50 | Source: ChemAnalyst, Intratec, ICIS pricing data (Q4 2025 - Q1 2026)
- **Price confidence**: High
- **Notes**: Current pricing as of Q1 2026: South America ~$1.23/kg, U.S. ~$1.40/kg (CFR Texas). Prices showed decline through Q4 2025 due to high inventories and subdued industrial demand from coatings, automotive refinish, and printing inks sectors. Year-end restocking and maintenance expected to support price recovery in early 2026.
