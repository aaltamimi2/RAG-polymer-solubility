## Additional Information
- **Physical properties**: Clear colorless liquid with pleasant odor; MW 94.54 g/mol; Water soluble; Flammable; May be light-sensitive
- **Toxicity**: Poison by ingestion; Moderately toxic by inhalation and skin contact; Skin irritant and severe eye irritant; Regulated for health and environmental effects
- **Chemical behavior**: Reacts with oxidizing agents; Undergoes hydrolysis in aqueous buffers
- **Industrial role**: Key intermediate in chlorohydrin process for propylene oxide (PO) production. PO used in plastics/polymers. Process involves: (1) synthesis from propylene + chlorine in water, (2) dehydrochlorination with base to form propylene oxide
- **Synonyms**: β-Chloropropyl alcohol, propylene chlorohydrin, 1-hydroxy-2-chloropropane
