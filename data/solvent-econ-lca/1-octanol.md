# 1-Octanol

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 111-87-5
- **Common names**: n-octanol, octan-1-ol, octyl alcohol, caprylic alcohol, n-octyl alcohol
- **Chemical class**: Primary fatty alcohol (C8)

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $1.50 - $3.00 (estimated) | Source: Estimated from regional market reports; Shandong ex-factory pricing ~11,750-11,800 CNY/ton (~$1.62-1.63/kg at Feb 2025 exchange rates)
- **Price confidence**: Medium
- **Notes**: 1-Octanol is produced both petrochemically and from natural fatty alcohol sources. Pricing varies by source (petro vs. oleochemical), purity, and region. Asian octanol market forecast to reach 3.6M tons and $6.8B by 2035 (average ~$1.89/kg). Industrial bulk pricing typically $1.50-3.00/kg depending on grade and volume.

## Environmental Impact Factors (LCA)
- **GWP (kg CO₂-eq/kg)**: 2.0 - 5.3 | Source: Literature comparison of petro-derived (~2.97 kg CO₂-eq) vs. palm kernel oil-derived (~5.27 kg CO₂-eq) fatty alcohols
- **HTC (CTUh/kg)**: Low (favorable) | Source: EPA Safer Choice assessment indicates C6-C12 alcohols including 1-octanol are low concern; specific USEtox CTUh value not publicly available
- **HTNC (CTUh/kg)**: Low (favorable) | Source: EPA assessment; 1-octanol log Kow = 3.15 indicates moderate bioaccumulation potential; rapidly biodegradable
- **ETOX (CTUe/kg)**: Low (favorable) | Source: Rapidly biodegradable at environmentally relevant concentrations; low ecotoxicity expected
- **LCA confidence**: Medium-High
- **LCA methodology**: Published LCA studies comparing oleochemical vs. petrochemical fatty alcohol production (ISO 14040/14044); ecoinvent 3.x includes fatty alcohol datasets; USEtox characterization factors require database access

## Summary
1-Octanol is a C8 fatty alcohol used as a solvent, defoamer, fragrance intermediate, and lubricant additive. Bulk pricing is estimated at $1.50-3.00/kg based on Asian market data and ex-factory quotes. GWP ranges from 2.0-5.3 kg CO₂-eq/kg depending on feedstock (petrochemical lower than palm-derived). Environmental profile is relatively favorable with rapid biodegradation and low toxicity concerns per EPA assessments. More complete than other C6+ alcohols due to established fatty alcohol LCA literature.
