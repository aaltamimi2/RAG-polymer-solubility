# Methyl Acetate

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 79-20-9
- **Common names**: Methyl acetate, methyl ethanoate
- **Chemical class**: Acetate ester

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $1.00-1.40 | Source: Bulk suppliers (2024-2025 data)
- **Price confidence**: Medium
- **Notes**: Bulk pricing $1,000-1,400/MT (≈$1.00-1.40/kg) with 10-15 MT minimum orders. Supplier quotes: $1,100/MT with 14 MT MOQ. Market valued at $231M (2024), projected to reach $388.5M-451M by 2030-2032 (CAGR 7.9-8.8%). Asia-Pacific dominates with >55% market share driven by China and India industrialization. Moderate-volume specialty solvent with growing demand.

## Summary
Methyl acetate is a volatile, low-toxicity ester solvent used in adhesives, paints, and nail polish removers. Growing market with moderate pricing. However, it poses developmental toxicity risks (metabolizes to methanol) and is proposed as toxic under Canadian EPA. Limited comprehensive LCA data available.
