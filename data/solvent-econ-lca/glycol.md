# Ethylene Glycol

- **Common names**: Ethylene glycol, monoethylene glycol (MEG), 1,2-ethanediol, ethane-1,2-diol
- **Chemical class**: Aliphatic diol, polyhydric alcohol

## Summary
Ethylene glycol is a widely-used commodity chemical produced from ethylene oxide, with bulk pricing around $0.50-0.65/kg in 2026. Its global warming potential ranges from 1.28 to 7.29 kg CO₂-eq/kg depending on the production feedstock, with ethane-based routes offering the lowest carbon footprint.
