## Summary
2-Methyl-2-butanol (tert-amyl alcohol, CAS 75-85-4) is a tertiary C5 alcohol used as a solvent in paint, ink, adhesives, and as a chemical intermediate for plasticizers and pharmaceuticals. Bulk pricing is estimated at $1.00-1.60/kg based on supplier quotes and TBA analogues. Environmental LCA data exists in Ecoinvent but requires database access; toxicity data shows acute oral LD50 of 1-2 g/kg (rat) and classification as Acute Tox. 4, Eye Dam. 1, Flam. Liq. 2.
