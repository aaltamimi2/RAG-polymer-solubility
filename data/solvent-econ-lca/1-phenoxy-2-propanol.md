## Summary
1-Phenoxy-2-propanol is a specialty slow-evaporating, hydrophobic glycol ether used as a coalescent and carrier solvent in high-performance coatings, textile dyes, and metal working fluids. It comprises >85% 1-phenoxy-propan-2-ol and <15% 2-phenoxy-propan-1-ol. The compound shows rapid biodegradation in soil but has higher production carbon footprint than alkyl glycol ethers.
