# Triethylene Glycol

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 112-27-6
- **Common names**: Triethylene glycol, TEG, Triglycol, 2,2'-(Ethylenedioxy)diethanol
- **Chemical class**: Glycol ether, polyol

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $1.24-$1.69 | Source: [ChemAnalyst](https://www.chemanalyst.com/Pricing-data/triethylene-glycol-1532)
- **Price confidence**: Medium
- **Notes**: Southeast Asia prices at $1.24/kg (1240 USD/MT) in May 2025; North America and Germany at $1.69/kg (~1685-1688 USD/MT) in Q2 2025 and December 2024. Prices rose 12.3% QoQ in Q2 2025 in North America due to supply-side disruptions and stable downstream demand. Market analysts expect elevated prices if crude oil prices remain high.

## Summary
Triethylene glycol is a colorless, odorless, viscous liquid (CAS 112-27-6) used as a desiccant and industrial solvent. Bulk prices range $1.24-$1.69/kg based on mid-2025 data with regional variations; North American prices rose significantly in Q2 2025. Environmental toxicity is low - TEG is essentially nontoxic to aquatic organisms and has low chronic toxicity. However, comprehensive LCA data including GWP and human toxicity characterization factors were not found in publicly accessible sources.
