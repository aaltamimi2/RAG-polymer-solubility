# Benzene

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 71-43-2
- **Common names**: Benzene, benzol
- **Chemical class**: Aromatic hydrocarbon
- **Molecular formula**: C₆H₆
- **Molecular weight**: 78.11 g/mol

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $0.59-0.93 | Source: ChemAnalyst, IMARC Group (February 2026)
- **Price confidence**: High
- **Notes**: Regional pricing (Feb 2026): Northeast Asia $0.93/kg, Europe $0.85/kg, India $0.81/kg, North America $0.79/kg, Middle East $0.76/kg, Southeast Asia $0.73/kg, South America $0.59/kg. Prices declined due to weak industrial demand, particularly in styrene, caprolactam, automotive and construction sectors. High stock levels and import competition adding downward pressure.

## Environmental Impact Factors (LCA)
- **GWP (kg CO₂-eq/kg)**: 1.76 (cradle-to-gate, fossil-based) | Source: Petrochemical complex LCA study
- **HTC (CTUh/kg)**: 5.5×10⁻⁷ | Source: USEtox model (emission to urban air)
- **HTNC (CTUh/kg)**: Part of total CTUh value above | Source: USEtox
- **ETOX (CTUe/kg)**: Not found (specific value) | Source: USEtox database (requires direct access)
- **LCA confidence**: Medium-High
- **LCA methodology**: Cradle-to-gate LCA data from petrochemical production (catalytic reforming, pyrolysis gasoline, coking by-product). USEtox model for toxicity characterization factors. Alternative bio-based and plastic waste-based production routes under development with potential GHG reductions.

## Summary
Benzene is a fundamental aromatic hydrocarbon with well-documented pricing and moderate LCA data availability. Fossil-based production via catalytic reforming has a GWP of ~1.76 kg CO₂-eq/kg, with human toxicity concerns well-characterized (CTUh 5.5×10⁻⁷). Market prices are relatively low ($0.59-0.93/kg) but volatile based on downstream demand from styrene and other derivatives.
