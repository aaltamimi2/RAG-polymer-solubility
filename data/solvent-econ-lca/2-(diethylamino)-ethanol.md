## Environmental & Safety Notes
- Classified for acute toxicity (oral, inhalation, dermal), skin corrosion, serious eye damage, and both acute and chronic aquatic toxicity
- Readily biodegradable with low bioaccumulation potential (log Kow = 0.21)
- Soil adsorption coefficient (Koc) of 5.98 suggests high mobility in soil
- LOAEC for respiratory tract irritation: 120 mg/m³
- Comparable eye irritation severity to ammonium hydroxide

## Applications
- Chemical intermediate for emulsifiers, detergents, and solubilizers
- CO₂ capture solvent with high chemical stability and degradation resistance
- Manufacturing of cosmetics and textile finishing agents
- Pharmaceutical intermediate
- Antirust compositions and resin curing agent
