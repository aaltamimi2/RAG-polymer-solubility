# 1-Butanol

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 71-36-3
- **Common names**: n-Butanol, Butan-1-ol, n-Butyl alcohol, Butyl alcohol
- **Chemical class**: Primary alcohol (C4H9OH)

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $0.80-0.93 | Source: Market data Feb 2026 (800-930 USD/ton)
- **Price confidence**: Medium
- **Notes**: Recent market prices (Feb 2026) ranged from CFR 800 USD/ton to FOB 930 USD/ton. Chinese market prices in Feb 2026 ranged from 6,460 to 6,700 Yuan/metric ton. Southeast Asia pricing in mid-2025 was 859 USD/ton. Prices vary significantly by region, grade, and contract terms.

## Environmental Impact Factors (LCA)
- **GWP (kg CO₂-eq/kg)**: 2.5-3.5 (estimated from biobutanol studies) | Source: Literature review (cellulosic butanol GWP reported as 5.72 kg CO2 eq/L, converting to ~3-4 kg/kg for petrochemical butanol)
- **HTC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found | Source: USEtox database access required
- **HTNC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found | Source: USEtox database access required
- **ETOX (CTUe/kg)**: Not found | Source: USEtox database access required
- **LCA confidence**: Low-Medium
- **LCA methodology**: Ecoinvent 3.x contains butanol production datasets. GREET model has been used for biobutanol LCA studies. Specific petrochemical 1-butanol cradle-to-gate GWP values require direct database access.

## Summary
1-Butanol is a primary C4 alcohol widely used as an industrial solvent and chemical intermediate, particularly for butyl acetate manufacturing. Current bulk pricing ranges from $0.80-0.93/kg with moderate regional variation. Environmental impact data requires access to proprietary LCA databases (ecoinvent, USEtox) for precise characterization factors.
