# Diethylene Glycol

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 111-46-6
- **Common names**: DEG, 2,2'-Oxydiethanol, Diglycol
- **Chemical class**: Glycol (diol)

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $0.49-$0.68 (bulk industrial) | Source: ChemAnalyst, Intratec, BusinessAnalytiq
- **Price confidence**: High
- **Notes**: June 2025 pricing: USA $0.58/kg (581 USD/MT), China $0.68/kg, Middle East $0.62/kg, Europe $0.66/kg. Year-over-year decrease of 17% in US market. Widely available commodity chemical with transparent market pricing. Used as chemical intermediate, solvent, plasticizer, and in antifreeze/brake fluids.

## Environmental Impact Factors (LCA)
- **GWP (kg CO₂-eq/kg)**: Not found (specific data unavailable in public sources)
- **HTC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found | Source: USEtox database search - no specific published values
- **HTNC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found | Source: No specific characterization factors in literature
- **ETOX (CTUe/kg)**: 365-25,000 mg/L NOEC range for marine species | Source: Ecotoxicity assessment studies
- **LCA confidence**: Low-Medium
- **LCA methodology**: Related compound (ethylene glycol) has ecoinvent data available. DEG metabolism produces glycolic acid, diglycolic acid, and oxalates associated with nephrotoxicity. Marine ecotoxicity characterized with NOEC 365-25,000 mg/L for indicator species (algae, crustaceans, molluscs, fish).

## Summary
Diethylene glycol (CAS 111-46-6) is a commodity glycol solvent with well-established bulk pricing around $0.50-0.68/kg (2025). While specific cradle-to-gate LCA impact factors are not readily available in public databases, ecotoxicological data shows moderate toxicity to aquatic organisms, and the compound is widely studied for metabolic toxicity pathways in humans and rodents.
