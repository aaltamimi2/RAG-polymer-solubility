# Ethane

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 74-84-0
- **Common names**: Ethane, C2H6, dimethyl, bimethyl
- **Chemical class**: Alkane (saturated hydrocarbon), Natural Gas Liquid (NGL)

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $0.16-$0.24 | Source: [Intratec Ethane Prices](https://www.intratec.us/solutions/primary-commodity-prices/commodity/ethane-prices), [EIA HGL Prices](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/hydrocarbon-gas-liquids/prices-for-hydrocarbon-gas-liquids.php)
- **Price confidence**: High
- **Notes**: US pricing June 2025: $0.162/kg (162 USD/MT, 11% decline from May). January 2025: $0.24/kg (10.9 cents/lb). Middle East pricing June 2025: $0.136/kg (136 USD/MT). 2026 projections: ~$0.25/gal contango curve. Ethane traditionally pipeline-transported to industrial consumers (not traded overseas like LNG). Primary use: ethylene feedstock (>72 million MT/year for ethylene production, 2023). US consumption forecast flat at 2.3 million bbl/day (2025-2026); exports rising to 630,000 bbl/day by 2026.

## Environmental Impact Factors (LCA)
- **GWP (kg CO₂-eq/kg)**: 1.3-4.2 (for ethylene production from ethane, cradle-to-gate) | Source: [ScienceDirect Ethylene LCA](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0960852425005310), [RSC Green Chemistry](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2025/gc/d4gc04538f)
- **HTC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found | Source: No specific USEtox data for ethane found
- **HTNC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found | Source: No specific USEtox data for ethane found
- **ETOX (CTUe/kg)**: Not found | Source: No specific USEtox data for ethane found
- **LCA confidence**: Medium (for ethylene production pathway)
- **LCA methodology**: Multiple peer-reviewed LCA studies for ethane-to-ethylene steam cracking. GWP for ethylene production from ethane: 1.3-4.2 kg CO₂-eq/kg ethylene (varies by energy source, heat integration, hydrogen handling). Well-to-gate (WTG) emissions: 1.05-1.30 kg CO₂-eq/kg ethylene. Ethane-based processes less energy-intensive (16 GJ/tonne ethylene) with higher yield (78 wt%) vs. naphtha cracking. Primary GHG driver: fossil fuel combustion for cracker furnace heating. Direct ethane combustion data available; toxicity characterization factors (CTUh/CTUe) not found in USEtox public databases.

## Summary
Ethane is a light alkane NGL with well-established bulk pricing ($0.16-$0.24/kg) primarily used as ethylene feedstock. LCA data focuses on downstream ethylene production (GWP 1.3-4.2 kg CO₂-eq/kg ethylene); direct ethane toxicity metrics not widely characterized in public LCA databases.

## Additional Information
- **Physical properties**: Colorless, odorless, flammable gas at STP; BP -88.5°C; MP -182.8°C; MW 30.069 g/mol; Sparingly water soluble
- **Combustion**: Releases 1559.7 kJ/mol or 51.9 kJ/g of heat, producing CO₂ + H₂O
- **Sourcing**: Natural gas processing (NGL component), petroleum refining associated gas
- **Primary use**: Steam cracking to ethylene (>72 million MT/year globally as of 2023)
- **Major consumers**: US, China, India, South Korea ethylene plants
- **Transport**: Pipeline-based to industrial facilities (not overseas LNG-style trade)
- **Market outlook**: US production and exports set new records in 2024; consumption flat 2025-2026 at 2.3 million bbl/day; exports rising from 530k (2025) to 630k bbl/day (2026)
