# 1-Propanol (n-Propanol)

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 71-23-8
- **Common names**: n-Propanol, propyl alcohol, 1-propyl alcohol, ethylcarbinol, propan-1-ol
- **Chemical class**: Primary alcohol (C3)

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $0.79-$1.41/kg (2025 market data)
- **Price confidence**: High
- **Notes**: Regional pricing variations observed - China: ~$0.79/kg, United States: ~$0.87/kg, Southeast Asia: ~$0.91/kg, Netherlands: ~$1.20/kg, Germany: ~$1.41/kg. Pricing reflects mid-2025 data with U.S. tariffs on imports affecting trade flows. Market valued at USD 5.63 billion in 2025 (CAGR 7.5% through 2030).

## Environmental Impact Factors (LCA)
- **GWP (kg CO₂-eq/kg)**: 2.0-7.1 kg CO₂-eq/kg
- **HTC (CTUh/kg)**: Data not found
- **HTNC (CTUh/kg)**: Data not found
- **ETOX (CTUe/kg)**: Data not found
- **LCA confidence**: Medium
- **LCA methodology**: Published LCA study on novel propanol production (Environmental Performance Assessment, PMC9354509). Range reflects different production pathways: 7.1 kg CO₂-eq/kg for conventional production, 2.0 kg CO₂-eq/kg with avoided flaring emissions in alternative methane-based pathway.

## Environmental & Toxicological Notes
- **Solvent ranking**: Ranked first in environmental impact among common solvents despite not being considered "green," primarily due to petrochemical production impacts
- **Combustion CO₂**: When used as fuel, combustion yields approximately 3.02 kg CO₂/kg fuel (lowest among C3-C8 alcohols)
- **Toxicity**: Moderately toxic by ingestion and skin contact; eye and respiratory irritant
- **Hazard classification**: GHS02, GHS05, GHS07; H225, H318, H336

## Summary
1-Propanol is a widely available three-carbon primary alcohol with well-established bulk pricing ($0.79-$1.41/kg depending on region). Life cycle assessment data shows GWP ranging from 2.0-7.1 kg CO₂-eq/kg depending on production pathway. USEtox toxicity characterization factors are not available in accessible literature. Market is growing steadily with significant regional price variations.
