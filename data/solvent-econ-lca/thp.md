## Physical and Chemical Properties
- **Molecular formula**: C₅H₁₀O
- **Structure**: Six-membered saturated ring with five carbons and one oxygen
- **Density**: 0.881 g/mL at 25°C
- **Boiling point**: 88°C
- **Melting point**: -45°C
- **Refractive index**: n₂₀/D 1.42
- **Applications**: Solvent for radical reactions, palladium-catalyzed coupling, Grignard reactions; THP group used as protecting group for alcohols | Source: [PubChem](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/Tetrahydropyran), [ChemSpider](https://www.chemspider.com/Chemical-Structure.8554.html), [Sigma-Aldrich](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/product/sial/293105)

## Summary
THP is a six-membered cyclic ether solvent structurally related to THF, used in organic synthesis and as an alcohol protecting group. Limited economic and LCA data publicly available. Characterized as non-carcinogenic, biodegradable, and safer than THF (non-peroxide forming), with potential for biomass-derived production.
