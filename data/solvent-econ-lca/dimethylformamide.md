# N,N-Dimethylformamide (DMF)

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 68-12-2
- **Common names**: DMF, Dimethylformamide
- **Chemical class**: Polar aprotic amide solvent
- **Molecular formula**: HCON(CH₃)₂ (MW: 73.11 g/mol)

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $0.54-2.00/kg (regional variation) | Source: Multiple market indices (2025-2026)
- **Price confidence**: High
- **Notes**: Significant regional price variation. Recent pricing (Oct 2025-Q1 2026): Northeast Asia $0.58/kg, India $1.22-1.49/kg, North America $1.70/kg, Europe $1.76/kg. China domestic prices range $0.60-0.63/kg. Industrial grade is less expensive than pharmaceutical grade.

## Summary
N,N-Dimethylformamide is a widely produced polar aprotic solvent (250,000 tonnes/year global capacity) with established pricing in the $0.54-2.00/kg range depending on region and grade. While ecoinvent LCA datasets exist, specific environmental impact factors (GWP, toxicity metrics) are not fully publicly available. DMF poses hepatotoxicity concerns and is classified as a possible human carcinogen (IARC Group 2B).
