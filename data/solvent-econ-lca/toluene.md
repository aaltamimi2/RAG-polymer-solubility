## Toxicity and Environmental Behavior
- **USEtox Framework**: CTUh measures human health impacts (disease cases per kg emitted); CTUe measures freshwater ecotoxicity (potentially affected fraction of species). These are calculated via USEtox model but specific toluene values require database access | Source: [LCIA Methodology](https://www.lifecycleinitiative.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/12/USEtox-the-UNEP-SETAC-toxocity-model-recommended-characterisation-factors-for-human-toxicity-and-freshwater-ecotoxicity-in-life-cycle-impact-assessement.pdf)
- **Human health effects**: Acute exposure causes eye/respiratory irritation at 100-200 ppm. Chronic exposure linked to neurological effects. Absorption via inhalation and dermal contact | Source: [ATSDR Toxicological Profile](https://www.atsdr.cdc.gov/toxprofiles/tp56-c2.pdf), [CDC NIOSH](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/npg/npgd0619.html)
- **Physical hazards**: Colorless, water-insoluble liquid with paint thinner odor. Boiling point 110-111°C, vapor pressure 22-26 mmHg at 20-25°C. Highly flammable | Source: [PubChem](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/Toluene), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toluene)

## Applications
- **Primary uses**: Precursor to benzene and xylenes; solvent for paints, thinners, lacquers, adhesives; gasoline additive | Source: [Sigma-Aldrich](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/product/sial/244511), [OSHA](https://www.osha.gov/chemicaldata/593)

## Summary
Toluene is an aromatic hydrocarbon solvent widely used in coatings and chemical synthesis, with bulk prices at 0.74-0.99 USD/kg (December 2025) showing downward trend. Conventional production has GWP of 1.32 kg CO₂-eq/kg (lowest among the four solvents studied). Specific human toxicity and ecotoxicity characterization factors (CTUh/CTUe) require commercial LCA database access, though USEtox framework data exists.
