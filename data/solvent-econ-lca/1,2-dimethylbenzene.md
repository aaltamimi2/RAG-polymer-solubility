## Summary
o-Xylene is an aromatic solvent primarily used for phthalic anhydride production (plasticizers, dyes) with bulk prices ranging $0.84-$1.14/kg depending on region. Environmental impact data is limited in public sources, with GWP estimated at ~3.3 kg CO₂-eq/kg for mixed xylenes; detailed toxicity metrics require proprietary LCA databases.

## Additional Information
- **Physical properties**: Colorless, slightly oily flammable liquid; BP 144°C; MP -13°F (-25°C); Specific gravity 0.88; Flash point 90°F (32°C); Not water soluble
- **Production**: Petroleum cracking affords aromatic mixtures; m-xylene isomerization produces o-xylene (net production ~500,000 tons/year as of 2000)
- **Environmental concerns**: Harmful to aquatic life; most releases are refinery losses to atmosphere during petroleum production, transportation, and processing; also released from automobile exhaust and solvent use
- **Applications**: Precursor for phthalic anhydride (dyes, indicators, polyester resin), insect repellent, plasticizers
