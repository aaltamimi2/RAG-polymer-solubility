# Isopropylamine

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 75-31-0
- **Common names**: Isopropylamine, 2-propanamine, monoisopropylamine (MIPA)
- **Chemical class**: Primary amine

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $1.08-1.58 | Source: Q3 2025 regional pricing data
- **Price confidence**: Medium
- **Notes**: Regional variation significant. China: $0.96/kg (955 USD/MT), USA: $1.58/kg (1575 USD/MT), Malaysia: $0.97/kg (970 USD/MT), Indonesia: $1.08/kg (1080 USD/MT). Small quantity pricing ranges $2.00-8.00/kg (1 kg MOQ). Bulk orders >1000 kg may qualify for 15-30% discounts. Price influenced by raw material costs (isopropyl alcohol, ammonia).

## Summary
Isopropylamine is an industrial primary amine used in pharmaceuticals, agrochemicals, and rubber accelerators. Moderate pricing with regional variation; highly toxic to humans (severe skin/eye burns, respiratory toxicity) and moderately toxic to aquatic life but biodegradable.
