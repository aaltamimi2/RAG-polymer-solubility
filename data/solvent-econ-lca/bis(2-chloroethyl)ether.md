## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 111-44-4
- **Common names**: Bis(chloroethyl) ether, BCEE, 2,2'-Dichlorodiethyl ether, Dichloroethyl ether
- **Chemical class**: Chlorinated aliphatic ether
- **Molecular formula**: C₄H₈Cl₂O (ClCH₂CH₂)₂O
- **Molecular weight**: 143.01 g/mol

## Summary
Bis(2-chloroethyl)ether is a chlorinated ether used as a pesticide intermediate, classified by EPA as a probable human carcinogen (Group B2) with significant respiratory and hepatic toxicity. Environmental persistence is low due to biodegradation and evaporation, with minimal contribution to global warming or ozone depletion, though it is designated as a marine pollutant.

## Additional Notes
- **Toxicology**: EPA Group B2 probable human carcinogen; oral slope factor 1.1 per mg/kg-day
- **Cancer risk**: Inhalation unit risk 3.3×10⁻⁴ per µg/m³; drinking water unit risk 3.3×10⁻⁵ per µg/L
- **Target organs**: Liver tumors (hepatomas), respiratory tract (most sensitive), kidneys
- **Acute toxicity**: LC₅₀ 5.73×10⁻⁵ M (CHO cells), one order of magnitude more toxic than bromate
- **Environmental fate**: Slowly evaporates from water/soil; biodegrades in water and soil; persistence <1 week in surface water
- **Atmospheric fate**: Low atmospheric persistence; removed by rain; no significant contribution to ozone depletion, global warming, or photochemical smog
- **Applications**: Chemical intermediate for pesticides, macrocyclic polyethers (dibenzo-18-crown-6, dicyclohexyl-18-crown-6)
- **Regulatory**: Marine pollutant; TSCA listed; limited industrial use due to toxicity concerns
