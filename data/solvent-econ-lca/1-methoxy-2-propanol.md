## Summary
1-Methoxy-2-propanol (PGME) is a high-volume industrial solvent used primarily in paints, coatings, inks, and cleaners. It is of low acute toxicity, readily biodegradable, and has a short atmospheric half-life (2.0-4.6 hours). The compound is completely miscible with water and widely characterized in environmental and safety databases.
