# Hexane

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 110-54-3
- **Common names**: n-Hexane, hexane, normal hexane
- **Chemical class**: Linear alkane, aliphatic hydrocarbon

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $1.18-1.82/kg | Source: [IMARC Group](https://www.imarcgroup.com/hexane-pricing-report), [ChemAnalyst](https://www.chemanalyst.com/Pricing-data/n-hexane-1151)
- **Price confidence**: High
- **Notes**: Q3 2025 regional pricing: Netherlands $1.18/kg, USA $1.68/kg, South Korea $1.82/kg, India $1.45/kg. Demand driven by edible oil extraction, pharmaceuticals, and chemical processing. Prices reflect bulk industrial grade; laboratory reagent-grade costs significantly more.

## Environmental Impact Factors (LCA)
- **GWP (kg CO₂-eq/kg)**: 1.0-2.5 (estimated) | Source: Petroleum refining LCA literature, [ScienceDirect LCA studies](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0959652619335309)
- **HTC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found; EPA classifies as Group D (not carcinogenic) | Source: [EPA Hazard Summary](https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2016-09/documents/hexane.pdf)
- **HTNC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found; RfC = 0.2 mg/m³ for neurotoxicity | Source: [ATSDR Toxicological Profile](https://www.atsdr.cdc.gov/toxprofiles/tp113.pdf)
- **ETOX (CTUe/kg)**: Not found | Source: Literature search incomplete
- **LCA confidence**: Medium
- **LCA methodology**: GWP estimated from petroleum refining cradle-to-gate studies. Hexane is extracted during crude oil distillation/fractionation. One study showed replacing hexane with ethanol in soybean oil extraction avoided ~10,600 tons CO₂-eq/year, suggesting hexane's production burden. USEtox characterization factors require database access.

## Summary
Hexane is a moderately-priced petroleum-derived linear alkane solvent ($1.18-1.82/kg bulk) widely used in oil extraction and chemical processing. Its production environmental footprint is estimated at 1.0-2.5 kg CO₂-eq/kg, with neurotoxicity concerns from chronic exposure but no carcinogenic classification by EPA.
