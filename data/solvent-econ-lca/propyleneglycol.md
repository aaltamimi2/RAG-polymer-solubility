## Summary
Propylene glycol is a versatile, FDA GRAS-approved diol used in food, pharmaceuticals, and industrial applications, with bulk prices ranging $0.85-$1.49/kg by region. Bio-based production routes (glycerol hydrogenolysis) achieve 40-81% GHG reduction versus petrochemical synthesis (6.34 vs 2.0-3.5 kg CO₂-eq/kg), with renewable routes showing superior environmental performance.
