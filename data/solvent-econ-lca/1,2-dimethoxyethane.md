## Summary
1,2-Dimethoxyethane (glyme) is a glycol ether solvent with relatively low volatility and toxicity compared to common organic solvents, making it more environmentally benign. Industrial pricing ranges $1.60-5.70/kg with significant market growth projected. However, no standardized LCA data exists despite its widespread use. Known hazards include reproductive toxicity (may impair fertility/harm unborn child) and effects on blood, kidneys, CNS, cardiovascular, and reproductive systems.
