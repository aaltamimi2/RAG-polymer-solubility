## Note on Isomers

## Toxicological & Environmental Concerns
- **IARC classification**: Related chloropropanols (1,3-DCP, 3-MCPD) classified as "possibly carcinogenic to humans" (Group 2B)
- **Toxicity mechanisms**: Chlorinated propanols show carcinogenicity, genotoxicity, and reproductive toxicity in animal studies
- **Target organs**: Kidney is primary organ affected by chloropropanol toxicity; chronic exposure causes nephropathy, tubular hyperplasia, and adenomas
- **Environmental persistence**: Chlorinated compounds generally show greater persistence; 1,3-dichloropropane found in 39% of U.S. groundwater samples
- **Hazard class**: UN2750 classification for dichloropropanols; Class 6.1 (Poisonous materials)
- **Decomposition hazards**: When heated, emits highly toxic fumes of chlorine and phosgene
