## Summary
Market size well-documented but specific unit pricing requires paid reports or direct supplier quotes. No publicly available LCA data found despite being a common industrial solvent. 1,4-Dioxane classified as "likely to be carcinogenic to humans" with liver, kidney, and nasal toxicity as primary non-cancer health effects. Low aquatic toxicity (LC50/EC50 >100 mg/L) and does not bioconcentrate readily. Ecotoxicity low relative to human health toxicity. Subject to regulatory restrictions in some jurisdictions. Use requires adherence to safety protocols and MSDS guidelines.
