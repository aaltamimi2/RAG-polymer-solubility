# n-Heptane

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 142-82-5
- **Common names**: n-Heptane, normal heptane, heptane
- **Chemical class**: Linear alkane, aliphatic hydrocarbon

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $0.05-0.15/kg (industrial grade, 95%+) | Source: [ChemAnalyst](https://www.chemanalyst.com/Pricing-data/n-heptane-1152), [IMARC Group](https://www.imarcgroup.com/n-heptane-pricing-report)
- **Price confidence**: Medium
- **Notes**: Q1 2025 prices showed regional variation with declining trends in Asia-Pacific (-4.14%) due to weak downstream demand from paints/coatings. Pharmaceutical-grade (99%+) costs $0.10-0.30/kg. Pricing directly tied to crude oil and naphtha costs.

## Environmental Impact Factors (LCA)
- **GWP (kg CO₂-eq/kg)**: 1.0-2.5 (estimated) | Source: [Ecoinvent documentation](https://support.ecoinvent.org/chemicals-and-plastics), petroleum refining LCA studies
- **HTC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found | Source: Literature search incomplete
- **HTNC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found | Source: Literature search incomplete
- **ETOX (CTUe/kg)**: Low ecotoxicity, no effects < 100 mg/L | Source: [EPA PPRTV](https://cfpub.epa.gov/ncea/pprtv/documents/HeptaneN.pdf)
- **LCA confidence**: Low-Medium
- **LCA methodology**: GWP estimated from petroleum refining LCA literature and Ecoinvent database coverage of petrochemical solvents (Sutter 2007). n-Heptane is produced via petroleum distillation/refining. Specific cradle-to-gate values require direct database access.

## Summary
n-Heptane is an inexpensive petroleum-derived linear alkane solvent with bulk pricing around $0.05-0.15/kg for industrial grades. Its production has relatively low environmental impact (estimated GWP 1.0-2.5 kg CO₂-eq/kg) and low ecotoxicity, though complete LCA characterization factors remain unavailable from public sources.
