# Water (H₂O)

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 7732-18-5
- **Common names**: Water, purified water, process water, deionized water, distilled water
- **Chemical class**: Inorganic oxide, polar solvent

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $0.0001-$0.01 | Source: Industrial estimates
- **Price confidence**: Low
- **Notes**: Process water costs vary enormously based on purity requirements, location, and treatment level. Tap water/raw process water: ~$0.0001-$0.001/kg; Purified/deionized water: $0.001-$0.01/kg; Pharmaceutical-grade water: higher. Capital costs for water treatment systems range from $80,000-$650,000 for 25,000 GPD systems depending on treatment requirements. Unit pricing highly dependent on source water quality, treatment train complexity, and local utility rates.

## Environmental Impact Factors (LCA)
- **GWP (kg CO₂-eq/kg)**: ~0.0003-0.002 (tap water); negligible for water vapor | Source: [Literature estimates](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11367-014-0799-x)
- **HTC (CTUh/kg)**: Negligible | Source: General knowledge
- **HTNC (CTUh/kg)**: Negligible | Source: General knowledge
- **ETOX (CTUe/kg)**: Negligible | Source: General knowledge
- **LCA confidence**: Medium
- **LCA methodology**: Water vapor has negligible GWP (-0.001 to 0.0005 on 100-year basis) due to rapid atmospheric removal via precipitation. For produced tap water, GWP primarily comes from pumping energy and treatment chemicals rather than water itself. Studies show tap water has 1/300 to 1/1000 the carbon footprint of bottled water. Treatment chemicals and electricity are the main LCA impact sources.

## Summary
Water (H₂O, CAS 7732-18-5) is the universal polar solvent with negligible intrinsic environmental impact. Process water pricing varies widely ($0.0001-$0.01/kg) based on purity and treatment requirements, with treatment system capital costs being more significant than water unit costs. GWP for tap water is minimal (~0.0003-0.002 kg CO₂-eq/kg) and primarily attributable to pumping/treatment energy rather than the water molecule itself. Human toxicity and ecotoxicity impacts are negligible.
