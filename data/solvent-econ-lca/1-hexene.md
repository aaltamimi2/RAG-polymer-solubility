# 1-Hexene

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 592-41-6
- **Common names**: hex-1-ene, alpha-hexene, hexylene
- **Chemical class**: Linear alpha-olefin (LAO), terminal alkene (C6)

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $1.50 - $2.16 | Source: IMARC Group Q2 2025 (US: $1.51/kg FOB Texas; Germany: $2.16/kg; Saudi Arabia: $1.56/kg)
- **Price confidence**: High
- **Notes**: Prices reported in USD/MT converted to $/kg. Q2 2025 US average was $1,507/MT FOB Texas. Market experienced oversupply conditions in 2024-2025, leading to downward price pressure. Pricing varies by region and purity (polymerization grade commands premium).

## Environmental Impact Factors (LCA)
- **GWP (kg CO₂-eq/kg)**: 1.5 - 2.5 (estimated) | Source: Estimated from ethylene oligomerization process; linear alpha-olefins from petrochemical routes typically 1.5-3.0 kg CO₂-eq/kg
- **HTC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found | Source: USEtox database access required
- **HTNC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found | Source: USEtox database access required
- **ETOX (CTUe/kg)**: Low (favorable environmental profile) | Source: OECD SIDS report indicates C6-C14 alpha-olefins degrade rapidly in soil, water, and atmosphere; expected low ecotoxicity
- **LCA confidence**: Medium
- **LCA methodology**: Estimated from ethylene oligomerization LCA studies; specific ecoinvent 3.x data for 1-hexene not publicly available. Environmental biodegradation data from OECD assessments.

## Summary
1-Hexene is a C6 linear alpha-olefin produced primarily via ethylene trimerization, used in polyethylene co-monomer applications and specialty chemicals. Bulk pricing is well-documented at $1.50-2.16/kg (Q2 2025) with regional variations. Environmental profile is relatively favorable with rapid biodegradation; GWP estimated at 1.5-2.5 kg CO₂-eq/kg for petrochemical production. USEtox toxicity characterization factors not publicly available.
