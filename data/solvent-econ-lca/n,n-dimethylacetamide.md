- **Common names**: DMAc, DMAC, Dimethylacetamide

In comparative polyurethane coating LCA (ecoinvent v3.8, SimaPro v9.4), NMP process showed higher GWP than alternatives, suggesting DMAc may have similar impacts. DMAc is classified as toxic for reproduction (after DMF) with hepatotoxicity as main health concern. USEtox model includes DMAc in solvent assessment studies but specific characterization factors not published in search results.

## Summary
N,N-Dimethylacetamide is a polar aprotic solvent widely used in polymer and fiber industries. Bulk industrial pricing was not found through public sources. It is recognized as hepatotoxic and toxic for reproduction, with relatively low ecotoxicity to aquatic organisms, though comprehensive cradle-to-gate LCA impact factors were not available from literature searches.
