# Cyclohexane

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 110-82-7
- **Common names**: Cyclohexane, hexamethylene, hexahydrobenzene
- **Chemical class**: Cycloalkane, aliphatic hydrocarbon

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $0.88-1.14 | Source: businessanalytiq & IMARC (US: $0.95/kg Apr 2025; Europe: $0.90/kg; Asia: $0.88/kg; India: $1.14/kg)
- **Price confidence**: Medium-High
- **Notes**: Recent 2025 pricing shows US at $953 USD/MT ($0.95/kg) in April 2025, with regional variations: Europe $896/MT ($0.90/kg), Middle East $1,010/MT ($1.01/kg), China $1,040/MT ($1.04/kg). Industrial Cyclohexane Market valued at USD 23,130 million in 2025, expected to reach USD 33,630 million by 2034. Used in nylon production (via oxidation to cyclohexanol/cyclohexanone), as solvent, paint remover.
