## Summary
1-Ethoxy-2-propanol acetate (propylene glycol ethyl ether acetate, PEA) is a low-toxicity advanced industrial solvent with excellent performance and strong solubility, used in paints, inks, and coatings for various polymers including aminomethyl ester, vinyl, polyester, cellulose acetate, alkyd resin, acrylic resin, and epoxy resin.
