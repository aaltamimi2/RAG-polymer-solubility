# Tetrahydrofuran (THF)

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 109-99-9
- **Common names**: THF, oxolane, tetramethylene oxide, 1,4-epoxybutane
- **Chemical class**: Cyclic ether, heterocyclic compound

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: 1.59 - 2.50 USD/kg (regional variation, October 2025) | Source: [IMARC Group](https://www.imarcgroup.com/tetrahydrofuran-pricing-report), [ChemAnalyst](https://www.chemanalyst.com/Pricing-data/tetrahydrofuran-1213)
  - Northeast Asia: 1.59 USD/kg
  - Europe: 2.30 USD/kg
  - North America: 2.50 USD/kg
- **Price confidence**: High
- **Notes**: Prices influenced by feedstock butanediol availability, downstream demand from spandex, coatings, and adhesive sectors. Europe and North America command premium pricing. Weak industrial activity and reduced automotive/coating demand drove price declines in late 2025.

## Environmental Impact Factors (LCA)
- **GWP (kg CO₂-eq/kg)**: 8.0 (conventional petrochemical route) | 5.51 (alternate estimate) | Source: [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/295078863_Environmental_analysis_of_the_life_cycle_emissions_of_2-methyl_tetrahydrofuran_solvent_manufactured_from_renewable_resources), [Green Solvent Selection](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/102544/1/art_10.1186_s40508_016_0051_z.pdf)
  - Bio-based 2-methyl-THF alternative: 2.9 kg CO₂-eq/kg (64% reduction)
  - Renewable ecoMeTHF™: ~0.15 kg CO₂-eq/kg (97% reduction vs conventional)
- **HTC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found | Source: No specific characterization factors available
- **HTNC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found | Source: No specific characterization factors available
- **ETOX (CTUe/kg)**: Not found | Source: No specific characterization factors available
- **LCA confidence**: Medium-High (for GWP only)
- **LCA methodology**: Cradle-to-gate analysis; bio-based alternatives studied extensively. Data available in [ecoinvent database](https://ecoinvent.org/database/). Renewable THF shows 97% emission reduction via biomass conversion.

## Toxicity and Environmental Behavior
- **Acute toxicity**: Low toxicity by inhalation and ingestion; severe eye irritant, mild skin irritant, not a skin sensitizer | Source: [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24148014/), [EPA Toxicological Review](https://ordspub.epa.gov/ords/eims/eimscomm.getfile?p_download_id=468515)
- **Ecotoxicity**: Does not present ecotoxicity hazard. Fish (Fathead Minnow) LC50 = 2160 mg/L; Water flea (Daphnia) EC50 = 5930 mg/L | Source: [NIOSH](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/idlh/109999.html), [OSHA](https://www.osha.gov/chemicaldata/107)
- **Environmental fate**: Inherently biodegradable, not environmentally persistent; BCF value of 1 suggests no bioconcentration in aquatic organisms | Source: [Monument Chemical SDS](https://monumentchemical.com/uploads/files/SDS/THF_-_SDS_(EU).pdf?v=1646684893115)
- **Hazards**: Highly flammable; tendency to form explosive 2-hydroperoxytetrahydrofuran upon air exposure; chronic exposure suspected of causing cancer | Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrahydrofuran), [PubChem](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/Tetrahydrofuran)

## Summary
THF is a versatile cyclic ether solvent used in polymer production and organometallic chemistry, priced at 1.59-2.50 USD/kg (October 2025). Conventional production has GWP of 5.51-8.0 kg CO₂-eq/kg, but bio-based alternatives reduce emissions by 64-97%. Low acute toxicity and inherently biodegradable, though flammable and requires careful handling.
