## Summary
Diphenyl ether is a low-melting aromatic ether (MP 27.8°C, BP 258°C) used in heat transfer applications, perfumes, and flame retardant synthesis. Bulk pricing data is not publicly available. Environmental impact assessment is limited, though ecotoxicity studies indicate low concern for aquatic environments at current use levels. Note: Brominated derivatives (PBDEs) have much higher toxicity concerns than the parent compound.
