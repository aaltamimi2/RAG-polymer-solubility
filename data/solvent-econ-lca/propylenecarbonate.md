# Propylene Carbonate

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 108-32-7
- **Common names**: 1,2-Propanediol cyclic carbonate, 4-Methyl-1,3-dioxolan-2-one
- **Chemical class**: Cyclic carbonate ester

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $0.59-$0.77 | Source: ChemAnalyst Q4 2025, OpenPR Q4 2025
- **Price confidence**: High
- **Notes**: Regional pricing (Q4 2025): USA $0.596/kg, Germany $0.742/kg, Japan $0.768/kg, Saudi Arabia $0.691/kg, Brazil $0.674/kg. Prices decreased from Q3 2025 ($0.608/kg USA) as demand from lithium-ion battery electrolytes and specialty solvents moderated. Adequate domestic supply and stable production rates reduced procurement urgency.

## Summary
Propylene carbonate is a commercial solvent and electrolyte component produced from CO₂ and propylene oxide, with bulk prices ranging $0.59-$0.77/kg depending on region. Environmental performance varies significantly by production route (chlorohydrin vs HPPO process), with synthesis stage identified as the primary environmental hotspot requiring optimization.
