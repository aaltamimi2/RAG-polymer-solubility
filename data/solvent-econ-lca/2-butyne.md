## Summary
2-Butyne is an internal alkyne used primarily in petrochemical research as a model compound for catalyst testing, and in pharmaceutical/materials synthesis. It is a colorless, volatile, pungent, and highly flammable liquid (bp ~27°C). No public industrial pricing or standardized LCA data is available for this specialty chemical.
