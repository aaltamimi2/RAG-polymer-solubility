# Cyclopentyl Methyl Ether (CPME)

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 5614-37-9
- **Common names**: CPME, Cyclopentyl methyl ether
- **Chemical class**: Aliphatic ether
- **Molecular formula**: C₆H₁₂O
- **Molecular weight**: 100.16 g/mol

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $10-15 (estimated, 2024-2026) | Source: Chemical supplier listings, market reports
- **Price confidence**: Medium
- **Notes**: CPME has been commercially available from Zeon Corporation since November 2005. Market reports indicate strong demand growth, particularly from semiconductor manufacturers in Asia Pacific (9.5% year-over-year price increase). Prices are expected to stabilize post-2026 as new production capacity comes online in India and Southeast Asia. In April 2024, Zeon Corporation announced a $25 million investment to expand high-purity CPME production. Small laboratory quantities listed at approximately $10/kg; bulk industrial pricing typically lower but not publicly disclosed.

## Summary
CPME is a hydrophobic ether solvent developed by Zeon Corporation as a "green" alternative to THF, MTBE, and dioxane, featuring low acute toxicity and negative mutagenicity. However, recent LCA studies reveal a carbon footprint of ~2 tons CO₂/ton CPME due to energy-intensive separation processes (28 GJ/ton heating duty), challenging its "green solvent" designation from an environmental perspective.

## Additional Notes
- **Toxicology**: Low acute/subchronic toxicity with moderate irritation; negative mutagenicity and negative skin sensitization
- **Energy requirements**: Separation process requires ~680 kg natural gas per ton CPME, releasing ~1.9 tons CO₂
- **Market**: Major manufacturers include Zeon (Japan), Zhejiang Alpharm Chemical Technology (China), Zibo Liantan Chemical (China), and Lianyungang JM Bioscience (China)
- **Regulatory**: TSCA approved (US) and ELINCS listed (Europe)
