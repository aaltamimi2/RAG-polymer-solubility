# Ethyl Acetate

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 141-78-6
- **Common names**: Ethyl acetate, acetic acid ethyl ester
- **Chemical class**: Ester (carboxylic acid ester)
- **Molecular formula**: C4H8O2
- **Molecular weight**: 88.11 g/mol

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $0.81-1.13 depending on region | Source: IMARC Group, ChemAnalyst pricing reports (January 2026)
- **Price confidence**: High
- **Notes**: Regional pricing (January 2026): Northeast Asia $0.81/kg (lowest), Middle East $0.87/kg, Africa $0.92/kg, India $0.97/kg, South America $0.98/kg, Europe $1.06/kg, North America $1.13/kg (highest). South America saw 17.6% decline Sept-Dec 2025. Pricing reflects supply-demand dynamics and currency fluctuations.

## Environmental Impact Factors (LCA)
- **GWP (kg CO₂-eq/kg)**: 1.0-2.5 (estimated from extraction solvent LCA study) | Source: Biotechnology for Biofuels (2020) reports 1000-2500 kg CO₂-eq per tonne acetic acid when using ethyl acetate extraction, suggesting 1.0-2.5 kg CO₂-eq/kg ethyl acetate
- **HTC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found | Source: USEtox database contains characterization factors but not publicly accessible; EPA PPRTV indicates systemic toxicity at 0.9 mg/kg/day oral exposure
- **HTNC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found | Source: USEtox database contains characterization factors but not publicly accessible
- **ETOX (CTUe/kg)**: Limited data - LC50 220 mg/L (96h, fish) | Source: EPA and safety data sheets; CTUe characterization factor not found in accessible sources
- **LCA confidence**: Medium (for GWP); Low (for toxicity metrics)
- **LCA methodology**: Ecoinvent v3.6+ contains ethyl acetate production datasets (Europe, UPR system models). GWP estimate derived from bio-acetic acid LCA study using ethyl acetate as extraction solvent. IPCC methods and Climatiq platform provide emission factors based on Ecoinvent data.

## Summary
Ethyl acetate is a versatile ester solvent with regional bulk pricing ranging from $0.81/kg (Northeast Asia) to $1.13/kg (North America) in January 2026. Estimated cradle-to-gate GWP is 1.0-2.5 kg CO₂-eq/kg based on extraction process LCA; comprehensive toxicity characterization factors require database access.
