## Summary
Hexafluoroisopropanol is a high-cost specialty fluorinated alcohol ($99-130/kg) used in pharmaceutical synthesis and materials science. While powerful as a solvent, it poses significant environmental concerns as a persistent fluorinated compound with a global warming potential 188-195 times that of CO₂, high toxicity (reproductive and acute), and resistance to environmental degradation.
