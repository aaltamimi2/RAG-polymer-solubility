# Ethanol

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 64-17-5
- **Common names**: Ethyl alcohol, grain alcohol, ethyl hydroxide
- **Chemical class**: Primary alcohol
- **Molecular formula**: C2H6O
- **Molecular weight**: 46.07 g/mol

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $0.70-1.32 depending on region and feedstock | Source: IMARC Group, USDA AMS, ChemAnalyst pricing reports (January-February 2026)
- **Price confidence**: High
- **Notes**: Regional pricing (January 2026): North America $0.70/kg, Northeast Asia $0.83/kg, Southeast Asia $0.94/kg, South America $0.97/kg, Europe $1.09/kg. US plant pricing in early February 2026: $1.21-1.32/kg ($0.55-0.60/lb). Ethanol commodity prices reached $1.64/gal on Feb 6, 2026. Bio-ethanol typically priced higher than petroleum-derived ethanol.

## Environmental Impact Factors (LCA)
- **GWP (kg CO₂-eq/kg)**: 0.7-1.5 (bio-based, cradle-to-gate); 1.3 (fossil-based) | Source: Multiple LCA studies published in Int J Life Cycle Assess, Biotechnology for Biofuels (2021-2025)
- **HTC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found | Source: USEtox database contains characterization factors but specific values not publicly accessible
- **HTNC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found | Source: USEtox database contains characterization factors but specific values not publicly accessible
- **ETOX (CTUe/kg)**: Not found | Source: Standard ecotoxicity databases (USEtox) would contain this data but not publicly accessible
- **LCA confidence**: High (for GWP); Low (for toxicity metrics)
- **LCA methodology**: Ecoinvent v3.8+, GREET model, multiple peer-reviewed LCA studies using IPCC GWP100, TRACI 2005, ILCD 2011 methodologies. GWP varies by feedstock: broken grains (0.536-0.983), sugar beet/maize stover (0.7-1.0), general bio-based range (0.7-1.5), fossil-based (1.3).

## Summary
Ethanol is a widely used renewable solvent with well-documented cradle-to-gate GWP of 0.7-1.5 kg CO₂-eq/kg for bio-based production (lower than fossil-based at 1.3). Regional bulk pricing ranges from $0.70/kg (North America) to $1.32/kg (US industrial plants, Feb 2026).
