## Summary
1-Propoxy-2-propanol is a widely-used industrial solvent in household and industrial cleaners, degreasers, metal cleaners, paints, coatings, and inks. It is a colorless liquid with ether-like odor, completely water-soluble, and evaporates quickly. The compound is readily biodegradable, has low affinity for soil/sediment, and does not bioaccumulate or persist in the environment.
