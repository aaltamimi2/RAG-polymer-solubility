# 2-Propanol (Isopropanol, IPA)

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 67-63-0
- **Common names**: Isopropanol, IPA, isopropyl alcohol, sec-propyl alcohol
- **Chemical class**: Secondary alcohol

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $0.87-1.94/kg (2025-2026 industrial bulk)
- **Price confidence**: High
- **Notes**: Isopropanol showed significant price variation by region in Q3 2025: USA $1,393/MT ($1.39/kg), China $872/MT ($0.87/kg), France $1,385/MT ($1.39/kg), Brazil $1,082/MT ($1.08/kg). Peak pricing in June 2025 reached $1,940/MT ($1.94/kg). China market average on January 15, 2026 was 5,325 RMB/ton (~$0.73-0.75/kg). Prices are influenced by feedstock propylene availability, pharmaceutical/personal care demand, and logistics costs. Consumer-grade rubbing alcohol expected in USD 2.30-2.90 range per liter for 2026.

## Environmental Impact Factors (LCA)
- **GWP (kg CO₂-eq/kg)**: ~1.5-3.0 (estimated from literature, conventional production)
- **HTC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found
- **HTNC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found
- **ETOX (CTUe/kg)**: Not found
- **LCA confidence**: Medium for GWP; Low for toxicity metrics
- **LCA methodology**: Ecoinvent database contains isopropanol datasets with GWP100 (IPCC AR6) as primary indicator. Alternative carbon-negative fermentation routes from waste gas have been studied showing negative carbon footprint, but conventional propylene-based production remains dominant. Specific USEtox characterization factors not found in search results.

## Summary
2-Propanol (isopropanol, IPA) is a widely-used secondary alcohol solvent with well-established bulk pricing around $0.87-1.94/kg depending on region and market conditions. GWP estimates range 1.5-3.0 kg CO₂-eq/kg for conventional production, though specific ecoinvent and USEtox toxicity data require direct database access for precise values.
