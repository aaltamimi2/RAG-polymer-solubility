# Chloroform (CHCl₃)

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 67-66-3
- **Common names**: Chloroform, trichloromethane, methyl trichloride
- **Chemical class**: Haloalkane, chlorinated solvent

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $0.68-1.05 | Source: businessanalytiq (North America: $1.05/kg, Nov 2025); China: $0.23/kg (June 2025); Europe: $0.68/kg
- **Price confidence**: Medium
- **Notes**: Significant regional price variation. North American pricing around $1.05/kg represents mid-range, with Asian markets considerably lower ($0.23/kg) and European markets at $0.68/kg. Prices vary by delivery terms (Ex Works, FOB, CIF). Market experiencing volatility due to muted downstream pharmaceutical and agrochemical demand. Ample feedstock availability contributing to well-supplied market.

## Summary
Chloroform (CHCl₃, CAS 67-66-3) is a dense, colorless chlorinated solvent used primarily as a precursor to refrigerants and PTFE. Bulk pricing ranges from $0.68-1.05/kg with significant regional variation. IPCC provides GWP values for atmospheric chloroform (16-20.6 kg CO₂-eq/kg), but cradle-to-gate production LCA data remain unavailable in public databases.
