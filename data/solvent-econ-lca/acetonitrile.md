# Acetonitrile

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 75-05-8
- **Common names**: Acetonitrile, methyl cyanide, ethanenitrile, MeCN
- **Chemical class**: Nitrile (polar aprotic solvent)

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $1.21–$2.48 (varies by region, 2025)
- **Price confidence**: High
- **Notes**: Regional pricing (2025): China $1.21/kg (June 2025, 1212 USD/MT), NE Asia $0.99/kg (-2% trend), India $1.27/kg (Sept 2025, 1265 USD/MT), USA $2.48/kg (June 2025, 2477 USD/MT). April 2025 US price was $1.54/kg showing 19% YoY decline. Significant regional price variation; Northeast Asia consistently lower than North America.

## Environmental Impact Factors (LCA)
- **GWP (kg CO₂-eq/kg)**: 3.5–7.5 (conventional fossil-based)
- **HTC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found (specific value)
- **HTNC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found (specific value)
- **ETOX (CTUe/kg)**: Low acute toxicity (LC50 ≥700 mg/L for fish/invertebrates)
- **LCA confidence**: Medium
- **LCA methodology**: Published LCA studies (ACS Sustainable Chemistry & Engineering, 2024); fossil-based production: 6.55–7.46 kg CO₂-eq/kg; bio-based alternatives demonstrate 64% reduction (3.50 kg CO₂-eq/kg) and 78% reduction in fossil resource depletion.

## Summary
Acetonitrile is a high-polarity solvent widely used in HPLC, chemical synthesis, and pharmaceuticals. Bulk prices range $1.21–2.48/kg with significant regional variation. GWP for conventional production is 3.5–7.5 kg CO₂-eq/kg, with emerging bio-based processes offering substantial reductions.
