# Oleyl Alcohol

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 143-28-2
- **Common names**: Cis-9-octadecen-1-ol, octadecenol
- **Chemical class**: Unsaturated fatty alcohol, long-chain aliphatic alcohol

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $2.40-3.30 (regional variation) | Source: Research and Markets, ChemAnalyst (2024-2025)
  - Latin America: $2.40-2.65/kg
  - North America: $2.95-3.15/kg
  - Europe: $3.00-3.30/kg
- **Price confidence**: Medium to High
- **Notes**: Q1 2025 saw 10% price increase due to feedstock (beef fat, fish oil, olive oil) shortages (ICIS). Market grew from $1.03B (2024) to $1.08B (2025), projected to reach $1.52B by 2032 (CAGR 4.96%). Pricing strongly influenced by regional feedstock availability, with Asian markets showing lower costs due to raw material access and labor costs. Primary applications include personal care, industrial lubricants, surfactant manufacturing, and pharmaceutical formulations.

## Environmental Impact Factors (LCA)
- **GWP (kg CO₂-eq/kg)**: -0.2 to 5.27 (bio-derived vs petro-derived) | Source: Journal of Surfactants and Detergents, ACS Sustainable Chemistry & Engineering
- **HTC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found | Source: USEtox methodology applicable but specific values not published
- **HTNC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found | Source: Data not available
- **ETOX (CTUe/kg)**: Not found | Source: Data not available
- **LCA confidence**: Medium
- **LCA methodology**: Cradle-to-gate LCA studies (ReCiPe Midpoint H, 18 impact categories) show oleochemical fatty alcohols from palm kernel oil have GWP of ~5.27 kg CO₂-eq/kg versus petro-derived ~2.97 kg CO₂-eq/kg when accounting for land use change. Bio-derived fatty alcohol from biomass feedstocks shows negative carbon intensity (-0.2 kg CO₂-eq/kg) or near-neutral (1.9 kg reduction vs fossil). Energy contributes <5% to GWP; primary impacts from feedstock production and land use. Studies use ecoinvent data for background processes. Note: ReCiPe method by default does not credit CO₂ intake during plant growth nor penalize biogenic CO₂ emissions.

## Summary
Oleyl alcohol is an unsaturated fatty alcohol derived from natural oils (beef fat, fish oil, olive oil) with bulk industrial prices of $2.40-3.30/kg depending on region and feedstock availability. Cradle-to-gate LCA shows significant GWP variation (-0.2 to 5.3 kg CO₂-eq/kg) depending on sourcing pathway, with bio-based routes potentially carbon-negative and oleochemical routes higher due to land use impacts.
