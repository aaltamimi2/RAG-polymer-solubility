## Additional Information
- **Physical properties**: Colorless volatile liquid; MW 89.14 g/mol; Molecular formula C₂H₅CH(NH₂)CH₂OH; IUPAC name: 2-aminobutan-1-ol
- **Chemical behavior**: Strong base; should be stored under N₂ in absence of CO₂; Nucleophilic, reacts with electrophiles forming covalent bonds (acid catalyst such as H₂SO₄ facilitates reaction)
- **Applications**:
  - Pharmaceutical: Precursor for antituberculotic drug ethambutol [(+)-enantiomer used]
  - Emulsifying agent: Soap form for oils, fats, waxes
  - Gas absorbent: Acidic gases
  - Chemical intermediates: Various industrial applications
- **Market segmentation**: Industrial Grade (bulk applications), Pharmaceutical Grade (drug synthesis, high purity), Others
- **Regional growth**: Asia-Pacific expected highest CAGR due to rapid industrialization and growing pharmaceutical/agrochemical sectors
