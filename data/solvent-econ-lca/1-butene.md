# 1-Butene

## Identifiers
- **CAS**: 106-98-9
- **Common names**: But-1-ene, 1-Butylene, n-Butene, alpha-Butylene
- **Chemical class**: Linear alpha-olefin (terminal alkene, C4H8)

## Economic Data
- **Bulk price ($/kg)**: $0.81-1.62 | Source: Market data mid-2025 (regional variation: Northeast Asia $0.81/kg, US/SE Asia $1.02/kg, Middle East $1.62/kg)
- **Price confidence**: Medium
- **Notes**: Northeast Asia pricing stands at approximately $1/kg as of recent data. Significant regional price variation exists. First quarter 2025 showed downward trend in Asian markets. Prices decreased to approximately 1,020 USD/ton in US and Southeast Asia (June 2025). Highly flammable liquefied gas, typically sold under pressure.

## Environmental Impact Factors (LCA)
- **GWP (kg CO₂-eq/kg)**: 2.0-2.5 (estimated) | Source: Olefin production via steam cracking typically ranges 1.5-3.0 kg CO2-eq/kg depending on feedstock (gas vs. liquid)
- **HTC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found | Source: USEtox database access required
- **HTNC (CTUh/kg)**: Not found | Source: USEtox database access required
- **ETOX (CTUe/kg)**: Not found | Source: USEtox database access required
- **LCA confidence**: Low
- **LCA methodology**: Ecoinvent contains olefin production datasets. General olefin production literature indicates gas-based feedstock (0.98 tCO2-eq/ton) vs. liquid-based (2.01 tCO2-eq/ton). Specific 1-butene values require direct database access.

## Summary
1-Butene is a C4 linear alpha-olefin used primarily in LLDPE production, polypropylene resins, and C4 solvent production. Bulk pricing ranges from $0.81-1.62/kg with significant regional variation, reflecting feedstock availability and downstream demand. Environmental impact data is limited in public sources and requires proprietary LCA database access.
